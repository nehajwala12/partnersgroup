## Capital structure (ILLUSTRATIVE; Breitling's real debt terms are not public)
- Term loans CHF 1,020m equivalent (EUR and USD tranches; part due Oct 2028 [Public]); revolver CHF 150m, CHF 45m drawn (30%); cash CHF 92m; net debt CHF 973m. Liquidity CHF 197m.
- Springing first-lien net leverage covenant 8.00x, tested only when the revolver is more than 40% drawn. Actual 6.07x on covenant EBITDA; EBITDA cushion 24.2%. Interest cover 2.06x (cash interest CHF 77.7m).
- Downside cases (net leverage): US demand −15% → 6.89x; franc +5% vs dollar → 6.53x; Asia-Pacific −20% → 6.77x; all three → 7.72x. Base rates +100 bps = +CHF 10.7m interest.
