## 02 | Method and evidence

**One fact base, recomputed from the ledgers, read through four definitions.** CreditSwan does not take the monthly package at face value. It rebuilds each number from the general ledger, payroll, the HR position register, boutique point of sale, the wholesale ERP and warranty activations, applies the credit agreement's own definitions, and keeps every adjustment traceable to a ledger line or a source record.

| Step | Question | Output in this demo |
|---|---|---|
| Channel | Are dealers selling what they buy? | Sell-in against sell-out, weeks of stock by dealer group |
| Boutiques | What does a boutique earn at each age? | Vintage four-wall table and ramp-to-plan |
| Price | Is the brand holding its price? | Realised discount, pre-owned index, listings |
| Earnings | Which EBITDA is the right denominator? | Reported, maintainable, covenant and run-rate ladder |
| Stress | What breaks first, and when? | Three named downside cases and a combined case |
| Watch | What will we see on Monday before the quarter closes? | 27 monitors, W41 board, escalation owners |

Evidence carries one of four labels: **[Public]** with source and date, **illustrative model**, **scenario** or **unresolved**. Gaps stay gaps; benchmarks set ranges, not Breitling facts.

**Why weekly data matters to a lender:** a quarterly certificate says what EBITDA was; warranty activations, dealer stock and pre-owned prices say what it is about to be. Each W41 breach was visible 9 to 15 weeks before a quarterly report would have shown it.

**For a private credit reader:** a misclassified CHF 1.9m is 0.07x of leverage and 0.9 points of cushion to a lender, and about CHF 24.7m of enterprise value at 13.0x to an owner (Tab 25). The same recomputation runs on every borrower, which is the point of a credit-monitoring policy under the AIFMD II loan-origination rules that apply from 16 April 2026 [Public].

**Assumptions on this tab:** method only; S01–S12 records are simulated; no system connection or live monitoring is represented as completed.

## 09 | Company boutique versus partner boutique

**Public sources (full URLs in the Partners Group research file):** Partners Group AUM release (15 Jul 2026); Bloomberg via FashionNetwork on FY26 results and job cuts (11 May 2026); Gulf Business; Private Equity Wire (23 Feb 2026); Swisswatches (22 Apr 2026); S&P and Moody's 2025 actions via press; Bloomberg on CLO holders and the October 2028 maturity (31 Aug 2026); FH export statistics via Bluewin (17 Sep 2026); Osborne Clarke on AIFMD II; Partners Group private credit releases (Jul 2025, Oct 2026).

**The four EBITDA definitions.** **Reported EBITDA:** earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation from the general ledger, after all boutique, wholesale, brand, central and restructuring costs (CHF 148.6m). **Maintainable EBITDA:** reported EBITDA plus non-recurring items only; the valuation input (CHF 154.3m as sent, CHF 152.4m restated). **Covenant EBITDA:** the credit agreement's Consolidated EBITDA; reported EBITDA plus non-recurring items and run-rate cost savings capped at 20% and realised within 18 months (CHF 160.4m as sent, CHF 158.5m restated). **Run-rate EBITDA:** the owner's view, adding the 2025–26 boutiques at maturity, the US price increase annualised and brand launch costs normalised (CHF 171.0m; CHF 169.1m restated); not accepted by a lender.

**Other terms.** **Weeks of stock:** dealer inventory ÷ average weekly sell-out. **Pre-owned price index:** asking price of core references on licensed panels as a share of list. **Four-wall EBITDA:** boutique sales less boutique cost of sales, staff, rent and local costs, before central overhead. **Net leverage:** (debt − cash) ÷ covenant EBITDA. **Springing test:** a leverage covenant tested only when the revolver is drawn above a threshold. **Cushion:** the share by which covenant EBITDA can fall before net leverage reaches 8.00x. **Lease-adjusted leverage:** (net debt + 8x rent) ÷ (covenant EBITDA + rent).

**Assumptions on this tab:** S-records simulated; public sources as listed; definitions are this demo's, not quotations from any real agreement.
