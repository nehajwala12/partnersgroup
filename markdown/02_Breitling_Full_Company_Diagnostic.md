**The operating decision:** fix the network Breitling already has before committing more capital to company boutiques. Seven levers bridge illustrative covenant EBITDA from **CHF 160.4m to about CHF 184m** (+CHF 23.5m, worth roughly **CHF 280–330m of value at 12–14x**) without opening a single boutique. Company boutiques return about a third as much per franc.

## 02 | Scope, engagement design and evidence

**This is a whole-company diagnostic built on one fact base that the board, the lenders and the valuation committee all read the same way.** It covers channels, dealers, boutiques, price, the pre-owned market, labour, stock, brands, capital allocation and the credit agreement. The boutique is the basic retail unit, the dealer group the basic wholesale unit and the reference family the basic product unit.

Evidence grades: **high** reconciles from ledger to cash (realised discount from POS line items); **medium** needs a controlled test (labour scheduled to traffic); **low** is an open hypothesis (Gallet demand at full distribution).

The design screens all 102 company boutiques, all 134 partner boutiques and every dealer group, then goes deep on 12 boutiques opened since January 2025, the six overstocked US dealer groups and the three reference families where pre-owned prices are weakest. No network-wide benefit is inferred from a handful of strong boutiques.

**Why it is not a consultant deck:** each finding is a live monitor. When the September package arrived on 15 Oct, CreditSwan recomputed it on the credit agreement's definitions and found, within the hour, CHF 1.9m of recurring hiring cost booked as non-recurring (Tab 17).

| Comparability rule | Rule in this demo | Error it prevents |
|---|---|---|
| Sell-out | Warranty activation at the dealer, by serial number | Counting shipments to dealers as demand |
| Like-for-like boutique | Open 24+ full months at both period ends | Ramp growth shown as organic growth |
| Realised price | Invoiced price after all discounts, by line item | Treating list price as realised price |
| Non-recurring cost | A cost that does not recur; a role refilled within 60 days is recurring | Recurring hiring cost added back as one-off |

**The add-back test:** the non-recurring add-back of CHF 5.7m sat well under audit materiality and inside every size cap, which is exactly why the error survived. Only joining the restructuring cost codes to payroll and the HR position register shows that CHF 1.9m of it recurs.

**Restatement log:** every change records the old value, new value, cause, affected decisions and owner. On 15 Oct fact F-07 moved from CHF 5.7m to CHF 3.8m (Tab 17). History is never silently overwritten.

## 10 | Boutique labour to traffic

**The newest boutiques are staffed for the visitors they were supposed to have, not the visitors who come in.**

| Labour lens (illustrative) | Mature boutiques (pre-2023) | 2023–24 | 2025–26 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Boutique staff, % of boutique sales | 14.6% | 18% | 26% |
| Traffic vs plan | n/a | ~102% | 74% |
| Staff hours vs plan | n/a | ~100% | 100% |

**Boutique staff CHF 49.0m (15.4% of boutique sales, M14, watch at 15%):** advisers, boutique managers, after-sales desk and reception.

**Mechanism:** schedule adviser hours to the half-hourly traffic curve and move weekday mornings into evening and weekend events. Keep senior advisers and clienteling roles as they are, because they drive conversion and repeat purchase. Moving boutique labour by 60 basis points of boutique sales is **CHF 1.9m**. The pilot reads −0.6 points of labour with conversion unchanged.

**Overlap rule:** labour savings at the 12 newest boutiques are counted here, not in the ramp lever. Released hours can serve more clients or cut paid cost, never both.

**The restatement link:** the 14 roles back-filled after the cost programme were US and UK boutique advisers and managers (Tab 17). The cost is real and recurring; the lever is to staff to traffic, not to cut and rehire.

**Assumptions on this tab:** labour split and ratios illustrative; weighted, they reconcile to about 15.4%.
